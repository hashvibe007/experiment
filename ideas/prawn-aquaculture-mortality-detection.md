# Real-Time Mortality Detection for Prawn Aquaculture

**Date**: 2026-01-21
**Tags**: #aquaculture #iot #agriculture #india #monitoring #earlywarning
**Status**: Draft

## Summary

A real-time mortality detection system for prawn aquaculture farms in India that alerts farmers immediately when mortality events are detected, enabling timely intervention and preventing significant financial losses that currently go unnoticed until harvest time.

## Description

### What is it?
An early warning system for prawn farmers that monitors water conditions, prawn behavior, and mortality indicators in real-time, providing immediate notifications when problems are detected.

### Why is it important?
Currently, prawn farmers in India only discover mortality issues at harvest time, resulting in catastrophic financial losses. By the time they discover dead or dying prawns, it's too late to take corrective action. Real-time detection could save millions in losses annually and improve the sustainability of the aquaculture industry.

### What problem does it solve?
- **Delayed Discovery**: Farmers learn about mortality events weeks or months too late
- **Financial Loss**: Complete crop loss due to undetected issues
- **Lack of Intervention Window**: No opportunity to take corrective measures
- **Knowledge Gap**: Farmers lack real-time insights into pond conditions
- **Scale Challenges**: Difficult to manually monitor multiple large ponds continuously

## Potential Applications

- **Commercial Prawn Farms**: Large-scale operations with multiple ponds
- **Small-holder Farmers**: Affordable monitoring for individual farmers
- **Cooperative Models**: Shared monitoring infrastructure for farming communities
- **Insurance Integration**: Data-driven aquaculture insurance products
- **Research & Development**: Better understanding of prawn mortality patterns
- **Supply Chain**: Predictable harvest volumes for buyers and processors
- **Extension to Other Species**: Fish farming, crab farming, etc.

## Implementation Thoughts

### Technical Approach
- **IoT Sensors**: Water quality sensors (dissolved oxygen, pH, temperature, ammonia, salinity)
- **Computer Vision**: Underwater cameras with AI to detect dead/dying prawns and abnormal behavior
- **Edge Computing**: Local processing to reduce latency and connectivity requirements
- **Mobile Alerts**: SMS/app notifications in local languages
- **Dashboard**: Simple interface showing pond health status

### Monitoring Parameters
- Water quality metrics (continuous)
- Prawn activity levels (behavior analysis)
- Feed consumption patterns
- Visual mortality detection
- Environmental conditions (weather integration)

### Alert System
- Graduated alerts (warning → critical)
- Actionable recommendations (increase oxygen, adjust feed, partial water exchange)
- Integration with local veterinary/expert networks

### Business Model
- Affordable subscription model for farmers
- Partnership with government agricultural programs
- Cooperative deployment reducing per-farm cost
- Data insights as a service for industry stakeholders

### Challenges to Address
- Reliable connectivity in rural areas (offline capability needed)
- Rugged, waterproof hardware for harsh environments
- Low-cost deployment at scale
- Local language support and farmer training
- Power availability (solar-powered solutions)
- Calibration and maintenance in remote locations
